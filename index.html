Gestures of Care

What you get when you are admitted to a The Great Journey Hospital. Applies to policyholders with a medical plan that covers Hospitalisation. Terms & conditions apply.

🎁Gestures of Care on admission
Admission gift, fruit basket and similar gestures of care when you are warded.
💸Fee waivers
Admission Deposit, Admission Fee, Identity (ID) band charges, and COVID-19 screening test are waived.
⚡Priority admission
Priority admission counters, ticketing machines and exclusive lounges for Great Eastern policyholders.
🧸Kiddo Express Programme
A smoother, child-friendly admission experience for your children.
🚪Early discharge, less waiting
Discharge without having to wait for the Final Guarantee Letter - with or without a minimal deposit.
🤝Great Assist
Dedicated hospital support for you during your stay.
Important: These privileges are offered at The Great Journey Hospitals only, not at every panel hospital. They are subject to availability and may change; confirm with the hospital admission counter or your agent before you are warded. Eligibility depends on your policy contract - refer to your medical plan for the Hospitalisation benefit.
